# Code of Conduct

ComicK Tools is made by people, for people who love comics and anime. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives) to feel welcome and safe.

## What we expect

- **Be kind.** Assume good intent, and remember there's a person on the other side of the screen.
- **Be patient.** Not everyone is a programmer, and not everyone writes English as a first language.
- **Be constructive.** Criticise ideas and code, never people. Say what would make it better.
- **Be honest.** Credit others' work, and say so when you've made a mistake.
- **Respect privacy.** Never share anyone's cookie, account, email or other personal details.

## What isn't okay

- Insults, slurs, harassment or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Discrimination of any kind, including by age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender, identity, experience, nationality, religion or sexual orientation.
- Trolling, spamming, or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Using the tools, or encouraging others to use them, against ComicK's rules or to harm other users' content.

## If something happens

Tell the maintainer privately, on Discord at **@russhvith** or by email at **me@russhvithdesign.in**. Include what happened, where, and links or screenshots if you have them.

Reports are kept confidential. Depending on what happened, the response can be a quiet word, a warning, removing comments or code, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project is: this repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere you're speaking for the project in public.
